# Notice

This repository is not an official publication channel for Turkish legislation.
It is a community-maintained, machine-readable mirror intended for engineering,
research, civic-tech, and legal workflow use.

Law texts are sourced from official public sources. Prefer
https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/ for primary source material unless a more specific
official source is required for the text being changed.

Before relying on any legal text in this repository, verify it against the
official source and the relevant source date. Official sources may be corrected,
updated, repealed, or reorganized after a repository snapshot is created.

When contributing legal text changes, include:

- the official source URL;
- the date you checked the source;
- a short explanation of the changed law text or metadata.

This repository does not provide legal advice and does not claim official status.
